Practice 1 -- Zoo input schema (student-facing)
================================================

This file describes the FIELDS of `zoo_features.csv` so that you can understand the
input. It is deliberately NOT the original UCI `zoo.names`.

The original file also describes the target in detail. Practice 1's unsupervised
seal withholds everything about the target until you have frozen both clustering
decisions, so the original is kept out of the student material and is used only to
verify provenance and schema.

After the reveal stage, `zoo_labels.csv` gives you the class of every row, and you
may report anything you like about it.


FILE: zoo_features.csv
----------------------
101 rows, one per animal, in the order of the original source. Comma-separated,
with a header row. No missing values.

  row_id        integer   course-owned key, 1..101, in source order.
                          Use THIS to align rows. It is unique.

  animal_name   text      a display/name field, NOT a key. It is not guaranteed
                          unique: the official source repeats at least one name.
                          Do not join on it.

  16 predictive attributes, in this order:
     1. hair
     2. feathers
     3. eggs
     4. milk
     5. airborne
     6. aquatic
     7. predator
     8. toothed
     9. backbone
    10. breathes
    11. venomous
    12. fins
    13. legs
    14. tail
    15. domestic
    16. catsize

  All sixteen are 0/1 Boolean except `legs`, which is a small non-negative integer
  count. That single difference is why the practical asks you to declare a scaling
  decision and argue for it.


FILE: zoo_labels.csv
--------------------
Exactly two columns: `row_id` and `type`. Join on `row_id`.

DO NOT OPEN THIS FILE BEFORE BOTH FROZEN DECISIONS. The seal is graded, and the
notebook guard checks the order of your cells.
